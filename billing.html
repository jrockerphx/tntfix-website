Credit & Debit Cards

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover.

A 3.5% processing fee will be added to all credit and debit card transactions to cover processing costs. This fee will appear as a separate line item on your invoice.

Example: A $500 job paid by card = $500 + $17.50 processing fee = $517.50 total

Payment Terms

Payment for any outstanding balance is due at or before the completion of work. This applies to the final balance on every job, regardless of size.

Jobs Under $500

Payment for the quoted price is due upon arrival. In the unlikely event that additional charges are needed, they will be discussed and approved before any additional work is performed.

Jobs Over $500

  • All equipment, appliances, and/or supplies must be paid in full before work begins
  • 50% of labor costs are due before work commences
  • Remaining balance for the quoted price is due upon arrival for the final visit; any additional charges will be discussed and approved before that work is performed

General Policies

  • We do not offer credit or carry account balances forward
  • Third-party financing options are available through qualified lenders
  • Written confirmation of lender approval must be received before work is scheduled
  • Balances not paid within 5 days of job completion are considered past due and subject to a 2.25% monthly finance charge (27% APR) on the unpaid amount
  • Accounts remaining unpaid 30 days after job completion will be referred to collections; the customer may be responsible for any associated collection costs and fees

Returns, Exchanges & Warranty

Product Purchases

We encourage customers to purchase products directly whenever possible. This keeps you as the first-party purchaser for manufacturer warranty claims and avoids paying a markup on items purchased on your behalf by TNT Fix.

Product Warranty Coverage

For items TNT Fix purchases on your behalf, warranty claims are handled according to the manufacturer's terms:

  • Within 90 days of service: failures are replaced at no cost
  • 90 days to 1 year: if the manufacturer confirms coverage, we handle reinstallation for a cost-only service fee; if the claim is denied and the failure is unrelated to installation, replacement cost is the customer's responsibility

Workmanship Guarantee

We guarantee our workmanship — installation-related defects — for one year from the date of service, at no cost to you. This does not cover pre-existing site conditions, structural issues, water intrusion or moisture damage unrelated to our work, electrical or plumbing conditions outside the scope of service, acts of nature, pest damage, normal wear and tear, or damage caused by the customer, other contractors, or third parties after installation.

If a failure occurs and the cause is unclear, we'll inspect the installation and communicate openly about what we find before determining responsibility.

Satisfaction Guarantee

If you're not satisfied with completed work, contact us within 30 days so we can make it right — whether that means returning to correct the issue at no cost, adjusting the invoice, or issuing a partial or full refund, whichever resolution best fits the situation.

We believe trust is built through honest communication, not fine print. If something isn't right, talk to us — we'll work with you to find a fair resolution.

Financing Options

We understand that larger projects may require financing. We work with qualified third-party lenders to offer payment plans for eligible customers.

How It Works:

  1. Request a quote for your project
  2. Apply with a financing partner of your choice
  3. Receive approval confirmation
  4. Forward approval documentation to us
  5. We schedule your work once financing is confirmed

Note: We cannot begin work until written confirmation of approval is received from your lender.

Common Questions

When will I receive my invoice?

Invoices are generated upon completion of work and sent to the email address we have on file. You can also access all invoices anytime through the customer portal.

How do I make a payment online?

Log into the customer portal and navigate to your invoice. Click the "Pay Now" button to pay via credit/debit card (3.5% fee applies). For fee-free payments, use one of our preferred digital methods and mark your payment with your invoice number.

Can I get a copy of my quote?

Yes! All quotes are available in your customer portal. You can view, download, or print them anytime. Quotes are valid for 30 days from the date issued.

What if I need a receipt for my records?

Receipts are automatically generated with each payment and are available in the customer portal. You can download and print them as needed for your records.

Do you offer payment plans?

We don't offer in-house payment plans, but we work with third-party financing companies that can provide flexible payment options for qualified customers. Approval must be received before work begins.

What happens if I don't pay within 5 days of the job being finished?

Payment is due at or before completion of work. If a balance remains unpaid 5 days after job completion, it's considered past due and a 2.25% monthly finance charge (27% APR) applies to the unpaid amount until resolved. Accounts still unpaid 30 days after job completion will be referred to collections.

What if I'm not happy with the work, or a product fails?

Our workmanship is guaranteed for one year, and if you're unsatisfied with completed work, contact us within 30 days and we'll make it right. Products we purchase on your behalf are covered under manufacturer warranty terms. Full details are on our Terms & Conditions page.
