What to Expect

We shut off power at the breaker, verify it's off with a voltage tester, remove old devices, and install your new ones. Everything gets tested before we close up. Most outlets and switches take 15–30 minutes each. Light fixtures vary based on size and complexity—we'll walk you through any wiring quirks before proceeding.

How to Prepare

  • Have your light fixtures, GFCI outlets, or smart switches unboxed and ready (we bring standard outlets, switches, and plates)
  • Know which breakers control which rooms (or we'll figure it out)
  • Clear the area below ceiling work
  • Make a list of everything you want updated
  • Note any outlets or switches that don't work right
  • Have your Wi-Fi password ready for smart switch installs
