Complete A/C Care Package

Your A/C just ran all summer. Give it a full reset (tune-up, both coils cleaned, and a fresh filter) in one visit.

HVAC Services Approx. 2 hours $349 out the door Serving Phoenix Metro, Coolidge & Casa Grande

Why This, Why Now

Phoenix A/C systems run harder and longer than almost anywhere in the country. After a summer of 110° days and monsoon dust, your coils are coated, your filter is spent, and any part that is going to fail is already on its way. Fall is the best time to catch it: no heat emergency, no week-long wait, and your system goes into winter clean and ready for next summer.

Everything your system needs after a long Phoenix summer, in one visit: a full tune-up, professional cleaning of both the outdoor and indoor coils, and a fresh filter, so it runs clean through winter and is ready for next summer.

  • Trip to your home and setup
  • Full seasonal tune-up with our 22-point inspection
  • Electrical components, capacitors, contactors, and connections tested
  • Refrigerant temperatures and pressures measured
  • Outdoor condenser coil cleaned and rinsed; bent fins straightened where accessible
  • Indoor evaporator coil cleaned; condensate drain checked and cleared
  • Fresh standard filter installed, plus a filter fit and airflow check
  • Heating-mode test on heat pump systems
  • Written findings and recommendations before we leave

Not included: Repairs or replacement parts found during the inspection (quoted before any work); Specialty filters such as HEPA or electrostatic; Refrigerant recharge.

What We Check

Our RSI-certified technician performs a comprehensive 22-point inspection covering electrical components, refrigerant system, airflow, safety controls, and overall system performance. You'll receive a written report of findings and any recommended repairs before we do anything beyond the package.
