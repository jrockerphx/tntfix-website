DIY Mini-Split Install

Got a mini split you need installed? We'll take care of it — you supply the unit, we handle the install.

HVAC 2 - 3 hours $599 - $899 Serving Phoenix Metro, Coolidge & Casa Grande

What This Service Includes

  • Mounting and leveling the indoor head unit and outdoor condenser
  • Running and connecting the line set between indoor and outdoor units
  • Refrigerant line evacuation and charging (EPA 608 certified)
  • Connecting to an existing, accessible power source
  • System startup, testing, and a walkthrough of basic operation

What's Not Included

  • Purchase of the mini-split unit itself (customer-provided)
  • New dedicated electrical circuits or panel upgrades — this requires a licensed electrician; we're happy to refer you
  • Permits, if your jurisdiction requires one for this type of install (confirming this is on you, but ask us if you're not sure)
  • Unusually long or complex line set runs beyond standard accessible routing (may need a custom quote)
  • Concrete pad or platform for the outdoor unit if one doesn't already exist

Pricing

$599–$899, depending on install complexity — line set length, mounting location, and accessibility all play a role. We'll confirm exact pricing after reviewing your unit and the install site.

Best For

  • Homeowners who've already purchased their mini-split unit
  • Simple, accessible single-zone installs
  • Adding zoned cooling to a room without extending existing ductwork

Not the Best Fit If…

  • You need a new electrical circuit run first (let's get a licensed electrician involved before booking this)
  • Your install needs permits beyond what fits our handyman-designated scope
  • You're installing a multi-zone system (ask us for a custom quote instead)

What to Expect During the Visit

We confirm your unit and site are ready, mount both indoor and outdoor components, run and connect the line set, evacuate and charge the refrigerant lines, then test full operation before we leave. Most straightforward single-zone installs run 2-3 hours — line length, mounting height, and wall penetration affect both time and where your final price lands in the $599–$899 range.

Site Preparation

  • Have your mini-split unit fully unboxed with all included components on-site
  • Confirm an accessible power source is available near both the indoor and outdoor unit locations
  • Clear a path between where the indoor and outdoor units will be mounted
  • Let us know if attic or wall access is needed — please make sure it's clear and reachable, not blocked by storage

Pet Policy (required): All pets, dogs especially, must be secured in a separate room, crate, or fenced yard away from the work area before we arrive — this is required, not optional. Loose, barking, or jumping pets create a real safety risk and prevent us from focusing on the job. If pets aren't secured when we arrive, we'll ask you to secure them before we begin; repeated issues may result in rescheduling.
