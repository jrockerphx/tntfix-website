Ceiling Fan Replacement

$209 out the door: trip and first fan included

The trip and your first ceiling fan, swapped in place of an existing fan or light fixture on a fan-rated box, balanced, and tested. Covered patio fans rated for damp locations are the same price.

  • Trip to your home and setup
  • Removal and haul-away of the existing fan or fixture
  • Fan assembly and installation on the existing fan-rated box and wiring
  • Blade balancing and a test of every speed, light, and direction setting

Not included: The fan itself: customer-provided (preferred), or purchased by TNT on your behalf at item cost plus a procurement fee scaled to the item's cost; Installing a fan-rated box or new wiring (see New Ceiling Fan Installation); New circuits from the breaker panel.

New Ceiling Fan Installation

$299 out the door: trip, fan-rated box, and first fan included

The trip and your first ceiling fan where there isn't one yet, or where the existing box isn't rated to hold a fan. We install a proper fan-rated box and run wiring from the existing switch when it's accessible.

  • Trip to your home and setup

  • New wiring from the existing switch when attic or ceiling access allows
  • Fan assembly, installation, balancing, and full function test
  • Patch around the new box (paint is customer-provided)
