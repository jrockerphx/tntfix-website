Toilet Repair

$209 out the door: trip and first repair included, standard part included

The trip and your first toilet repair: running toilets, slow refills, phantom flushing, or a stuck handle, fixed one component at a time with a standard part supplied by us.

Shut-Off Valve Replacement

$209 out the door: trip and first valve included, standard valve included

The trip and your first shut-off valve: a stuck, dripping, or corroded toilet, sink, or appliance valve swapped for a quarter-turn ball valve so you can shut the water off when you need to.

  • Trip to your home and setup
  • One standard brass quarter-turn valve, supplied by us
  • Compression or push-fit connection (copper, PEX, or CPVC)
  • Leak test and operation check

Not included: Main water shut-off valves or valves inside walls; Re-piping or soldered connections.

More while we're there? Additional Plumbing Fix: $79 each.

Toilet Rebuild

$249 out the door: trip, complete tank kit, and wax ring if needed, all included

When several things are worn out, we rebuild the whole tank with a complete kit. If it leaks at the base we replace the wax ring too, but we don't pull a toilet unless there's a leak, so your flange isn't put at risk for nothing.

  • Trip to your home and setup
  • Complete tank kit (fill valve, flush valve, flapper, tank bolts, and gaskets), supplied by us
  • Wax ring replacement when the base is leaking
  • All components adjusted and tested

Not included: Flange repair (see Toilet Flange Repair Add-On); Cracked tanks or bowls (replacement recommended).

More while we're there? Additional Toilet Rebuild: $159 each.

What's Not Included

  • Troubleshooting time—if we need to diagnose an issue before fixing it, that's billed at $99/hour. Once identified, we'll discuss before proceeding.
  • Water heater installation or major repairs
  • Main sewer line work or rooter service
  • Re-piping or moving water lines
  • Work requiring permits or licensed plumber sign-off
  • Gas line work of any kind
  • Shower valve or tub spout replacement (behind-wall work)
  • Slab leaks or foundation plumbing

What to Expect

We shut off the water, verify it's off, and get to work. For toilet and valve work, we'll have towels down and keep things dry. Everything gets tested before we pack up—you'll see it working before we leave. Most single-item repairs take 30–60 minutes. Toilet replacements and disposal installs may run a bit longer depending on access and conditions.

How to Prepare

  • Know where your main water shut-off is (or we'll find it)
  • Clear under the sink or around the toilet
  • Have new fixtures unboxed and confirm all parts are present
  • Note any specific issues—leaks, noises, slow drains
  • If replacing a toilet, have the new one in the bathroom or nearby
  • Let us know if you have older galvanized or polybutylene pipes
