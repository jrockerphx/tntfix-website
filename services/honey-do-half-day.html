Common Half-Day Tasks (One Tech)

Here's what one tech typically knocks out in 4 hours:

  • Mount 1–2 TVs on the wall
  • Hang shelves, mirrors, or heavy artwork
  • Install 3–5 smart switches or outlets
  • Assemble 1–2 pieces of furniture (bed frames, desks, shelving units)
  • Adjust or rehang interior doors
  • Replace cabinet knobs/pulls throughout a kitchen
  • Install door stops, hooks, and towel bars
  • Fix running toilets or leaky faucets (1–2 fixtures)
  • Replace light fixtures or ceiling fans (1–2 units)
  • Patch and paint small drywall holes
  • Install weatherstripping or door sweeps
  • Replace smoke/CO detectors
  • Caulk tubs, showers, or windows
  • Install blinds or curtain rods
  • Minor screen repairs

What Two Techs Typically Knock Out

  • Mount 3–4 TVs throughout the house
  • Install ceiling fans in 2–3 rooms
  • Replace cabinet hardware in the kitchen and bathrooms
  • Assemble multiple pieces of furniture (bedroom set, home office setup)
  • Hang shelving, artwork, and mirrors throughout the home
  • Bathroom refresh (faucet, towel bars, mirror, light fixture)
  • Replace interior door knobs and hinges
  • Patch and paint multiple drywall repairs
  • Weather-seal doors and windows
  • Garage organization (pegboard, shelving, bike hooks)

What's Included

  • 4 hours of on-site labor per technician (clock starts when we begin work)
  • One skilled technician, or two with the Two-Tech option, each with a fully-stocked tool kit
  • Basic hardware and fasteners (anchors, screws, wire nuts, etc.)
  • Task prioritization help before the visit
  • Photos of completed work
  • Debris cleanup and haul-away of old items

Not Included

  • Parts, fixtures, or materials: customer-provided (preferred), or purchased by TNT on your behalf at item cost plus a procurement fee scaled to the item's cost
  • Tasks requiring permits or licensed contractor work
  • Major electrical (new circuits, panel work)
  • Major plumbing (water heaters, re-piping, sewer lines)
  • Structural modifications
  • Roofing or exterior work above single-story reach
