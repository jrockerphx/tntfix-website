Emergency No-Cool Service

Appointment windows are estimated to within ~2 hours — Phoenix traffic, distance from our last job, and Murphy's Law all get a vote too.

Emergency Dispatch ~90 minutes $449 Serving Phoenix Metro, Coolidge & Casa Grande

Pricing

We understand that emergencies don't happen at convenient times, and when they do, getting help quickly matters. After-hours or emergency calls are given our highest priority and are typically evaluated or addressed the same day.

  • Standard service call: $149 (covers diagnosis and evaluation, charged upon arrival — repairs are quoted separately and billed only once approved)
  • Emergency service call: $449 (after-hours, weekends, or same-day priority during heat emergencies)
  • Common repairs: $75–$350 depending on parts needed
  • Major repairs: quoted after diagnosis

Emergency Calls

Emergency calls are prioritized so we can help restore comfort as quickly as possible, even when that means interrupting scheduled or in-progress work.

To support immediate dispatch and on-call availability, a $449 emergency service fee applies to all emergencies we dispatch — during regular business hours, after hours, weekends, or holidays. This fee covers the rapid response and on-site evaluation, regardless of whether you choose to proceed with the recommended repair.

If your emergency can be handled same day, during regular business hours, without disrupting scheduled work, we will let you know and the emergency fee will be waived. Our goal is to get you cooling—not to create unnecessary expense.

Emergency requests submitted after hours, on weekends, or holidays are based on technician availability. We will make every effort to confirm availability and dispatch within one hour of receiving your request; however, availability cannot be guaranteed. For this reason, we strongly recommend contacting multiple service providers and not waiting on a single response.

If you have not heard from TNT Fix within one hour, please assume we are unavailable at that time and continue seeking service. Prolonged exposure to extreme heat can be dangerous, and your safety is the priority.

Temporary Cooling Solutions

If your system needs parts we don't have on hand, we can often get you partial cooling by bypassing failed components or adjusting system operation. We'll also provide guidance on using fans, managing sun exposure, and staying safe until full repairs are complete.

Site Preparation

  • Clear access to your outdoor condenser/compressor unit and indoor air handler
  • Let us know if attic access is needed — please make sure it's clear and reachable, not blocked by storage
  • Clear the area around your thermostat

Pet Policy (required): All pets, dogs especially, must be secured in a separate room, crate, or fenced yard away from the work area before we arrive — this is required, not optional. Loose, barking, or jumping pets create a real safety risk and prevent us from focusing on the job. If pets aren't secured when we arrive, we'll ask you to secure them before we begin; repeated issues may result in rescheduling.
