Thermostat Replacement

A reliable, no-fuss thermostat swap for anyone who just wants simple, dependable temperature control. No apps, no accounts, no Wi-Fi required.

HVAC Services Approx. 1 hour $209, trip included Serving Phoenix Metro, Coolidge & Casa Grande

What This Service Includes

Not everyone wants a thermostat that talks to their phone. If your old thermostat is failing, hard to read, or just worn out, we'll replace it with a standard manual, programmable, or basic Wi-Fi model and make sure your heating and cooling respond exactly the way they should.

  • Compatibility check against your system: single-stage, heat pump, or gas furnace.
  • Professional removal of your old thermostat and installation of the new one, with a trim plate if needed to cover old marks.
  • Proper low-voltage wiring verification and correction where needed.
  • Basic schedule set up for programmable models, tailored to your daily routine.
  • Heating, cooling, and fan test cycles before we leave.
  • A quick walkthrough of the controls so you're comfortable using it.

Pricing

  • First thermostat: $209, trip and setup included.
  • Each additional thermostat on the same visit (a second zone, for example): $99.
  • The thermostat itself: customer-provided (preferred), or purchased by TNT on your behalf at item cost plus a procurement fee scaled to the item's cost. Tell us your system type and we'll recommend one.

Good Fit For

  • Homeowners who want simple, dependable controls without apps or accounts.
  • Older thermostats with faded screens, sticky buttons, or unreliable temperature readings.
  • Rental properties where landlords want durable, tenant-friendly controls.
  • Homes without a C-wire where a battery-powered thermostat is the easiest option.

Not the Best Fit If…

  • You want remote control, voice control, or energy reports. See Smart Thermostat Installation.
  • Your system isn't responding at all, which may be a control or equipment issue. See Thermostat & Control Troubleshooting.
  • You have line-voltage (120/240 V) baseboard or wall heaters.
  • You need work that must be performed by a licensed HVAC contractor beyond thermostat wiring.

Before We Arrive

  • Have your new thermostat and any included backplate on-site (or ask us to pick one up).
  • Clear the area around your existing thermostat and HVAC equipment.
  • Know whether your system is a heat pump or a gas furnace, if you can (we'll confirm).
  • Think about your typical schedule if you're getting a programmable model.
