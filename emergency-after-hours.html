Three Ways to Get Help — Pick What You Actually Need

Not every "I need this handled today" moment is a true emergency, and we'd rather you pay the right price than the highest one. Here's the honest breakdown:

  • Standard Scheduling ($99–$149): Book like any other service. If we have an opening today or tomorrow during normal hours, this is almost always your cheapest option — check our regular HVAC services first before paying a dispatch fee you don't need.
  • Priority Dispatch ($249, daytime only): Want to get to the front of the line and it's still early in the day? This gets you bumped ahead of the regular queue the same day.
  • After-Hours Dispatch ($449): Evenings after 5:30pm, weekends, or holidays. This is genuine after-hours work — real overtime, real inconvenience, priced accordingly.

Our honest advice: if it's daytime and not truly urgent, check standard scheduling first. If our next standard opening is later today anyway, there's no reason to pay extra for priority. We'll never talk you into a fee you don't need — that's not how we want to earn your business.
